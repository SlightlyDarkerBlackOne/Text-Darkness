Perhaps I could wedge the pipe in the **{{style:emphasis|door's dent}}** and pry it open.

And that's exactly what you do. It fulcrums nicely against its own frame. You begin to apply pressure. The **{{style:emphasis|door}}** creaks, and the metal bends, soon with little effort, a {{style:emphasis|piece of the door}} around the **{{style:emphasis|dent}}** breaks off with a clank. The **{{style:emphasis|hole}}** is much bigger now. Not enough for you to fit through completely unfortunately.
